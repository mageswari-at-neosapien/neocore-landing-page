# **Final website design brief \- NeoCore for Enterprise**

## *Single source of truth for website design and development*

# **1\. Page mandate**

### **Objective**

Build a focused enterprise landing page that converts qualified buyers into conversations with NeoSapien’s enterprise team. The page must make one proposition immediately clear: NeoCore turns frontline sales conversations into centralized intelligence for pitch adherence, coaching and Voice of Customer.

### **Primary audience, in order**

1\. Sales Excellence, Revenue Transformation and GTM Excellence leaders  
2\. CROs, national or regional sales heads and business-unit leaders  
3\. Sales coaching, L\&D and Customer Insights teams  
4\. IT, data, security and procurement stakeholders validating deployment

### **Primary conversion**

Talk to our enterprise team

### **Secondary actions**

• See a sample analysis \- scrolls to the ungated synthetic example in Fold 4\.  
• See a tailored demo \- opens the same qualification form and is visually subordinate to the primary CTA.

### **Page-wide design rules**

• NeoCore’s intelligence, dashboards and management outputs must receive at least 80% of the page’s visual prominence.  
• Neo One is the low-friction capture input, not the central product story. Keep it to 15–20% or less of any solution visual.  
• Use real product UI wherever available. Label representative screens “Illustrative view.”  
• Avoid stock sales-team photography, device-led hero imagery and equal-sized feature-icon flows.  
• Keep the page to seven folds. Every fold must advance the story or conversion.  
• Use the current NeoSapien enterprise visual system: black or deep-green background, bright green accent, high-contrast white typography and restrained motion.  
• The primary CTA label is always “Talk to our enterprise team.”

### **Navigation**

NeoCore | How it works | Outcomes | Security | FAQ | Talk to our enterprise team  
Use sticky navigation on desktop and a compact mobile header with the primary CTA accessible.

Neo One captures frontline conversations with minimal added effort.

## **Fold 6 \- FAQ**

### **Objective**

Resolve the most common product, deployment and integration questions without interrupting the main narrative.

### **Representation**

Use a collapsed accordion. Open only the first item by default. Keep answers to two or three short sentences.

What is the difference between Neo One and NeoCore?  
Neo One captures frontline conversations. NeoCore processes, structures and aggregates those conversations into management intelligence for adherence, coaching and Voice of Customer.

Can NeoCore work across languages and company-specific terminology?  
Yes. NeoCore supports 100+ languages and can be tuned for product names, local brands, competitors, regional terminology and industry-specific language.

Can we define our own adherence criteria?  
Yes. Teams can configure the pitch points, questions, processes and follow-ups that matter to their sales motion. NeoCore structures captured conversations against those custom fields.

How does NeoCore improve coaching?  
It surfaces repeated behaviours, missed steps and execution gaps across representatives and teams, helping managers focus coaching on specific evidence rather than occasional observation.

Can NeoCore integrate with our existing systems?  
Yes. APIs and MCP can connect NeoCore with CRM, BI and other enterprise workflows. The precise integration scope is agreed during the pilot.

How is enterprise data handled?  
NeoCore is ISO 27001 certified, does not store audio by default, does not train models on customer data and uses India-resident servers. PII redaction and role-based access can be configured for the deployment.

What does an enterprise pilot include?  
A pilot defines the team, use case and success measures; configures company terminology and custom fields; deploys capture; measures results; and produces a rollout recommendation.

# **4\. Responsive, accessibility and performance requirements**

• Desktop content width: approximately 1200–1280 px with generous side margins.  
• Hero proposition and primary CTA must be visible in the first mobile viewport.  
• Stack flows, cards and sample-analysis panels in their intended reading order.  
• Do not hide essential information in hover states.  
• Avoid auto-playing carousels, autoplay audio and heavy background video.  
• Respect reduced-motion preferences.  
• Meet WCAG AA color contrast and keyboard-navigation requirements.  
• Add descriptive alternative text for product visuals and meaningful logo labels.  
• Use responsive WebP or AVIF images and lazy-load below-fold media.  
• Target Largest Contentful Paint below 2.5 seconds on a typical mobile connection.  
• Reserve image dimensions to avoid layout shift.
